# Code of conduct

MODENA welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities, experience,
and scientific viewpoints. This policy applies to project issues, pull
requests, reviews, and other spaces maintained for the project, and when
someone represents MODENA publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and assume good faith while checking evidence.
- Critique methods, results, and code with specific, constructive reasoning.
- Accept corrections and acknowledge the work and sources of others.
- Respect privacy and requests to stop unwelcome contact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable. Disagreement about a
scientific result is welcome; intimidation or hostility toward the person
raising it is not.

## Reporting and response

The maintainer, [@ricardoleal20](https://github.com/ricardoleal20), is responsible
for applying this policy. For a private report, use a private contact method
listed on the maintainer's profile, if one is available. MODENA does not
currently publish a dedicated confidential reporting address. If no private
method is available, you may open an issue requesting a private contact route
without identifying affected people or sharing sensitive details. Do not
post private incident evidence in public issues.

Include the behavior, relevant context or links, and any immediate concerns
once a private route is established. The maintainer will review the available
information, seek clarification where needed, and limit disclosure to what
is necessary to respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Responses may include a request to change behavior, removal of content,
a warning, a temporary restriction, or exclusion from project spaces,
depending on severity and repetition. Threats or severe harassment may lead
to immediate exclusion. People affected by a decision may provide new
information to the maintainer for reconsideration. This single-maintainer
project has no independent appeals body; GitHub's platform reporting tools
remain available for conduct that violates its policies.

This is an original, project-specific policy.
