## Approved icon prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Create ONE standalone premium abstract logo concept for MODENA, an open-source molecular dynamics engine written in Rust. Internal concept title: "Flow", option 2. Do not render any text.
Logo idea: numerical trajectories evolving through time. THREE bold continuous smooth paths sweep together through a tight half-turn, creating one compact fluid asymmetric emblem. The flowing paths share a precise spacing and form an elegant sculptural curved hairpin silhouette, like a contour-map stream bending around invisible geometry. The emblem has a distinct entrance and exit, creates interesting negative space, and feels dynamic but measured. NOT a letter, NOT an infinity symbol, NOT a ball, NOT an atom. One path mint and two ivory. Strong rounded band geometry with consistent widths, visible purposeful gaps, bold enough for a 32px icon. Beautifully balanced, an ownable timeless symbol for numerical motion. No point or particle at the end of any line.
Presentation: square dark petroleum #10262B background, large centered logo with generous 25% margins. Ivory #F7FAF8 and restrained mint #5ED8C1 only. Flat vector-like graphic, clean crisp silhouette, quiet Swiss graphic design meets scientific instrumentation. Absolutely no bevels or material rendering.
Must avoid: letters including M, text, central dots, circles as particles, arrows, orbit rings, molecular diagrams, cubes, hexagons, network nodes, infinity loops, lightning, sparkle, gradients, glow, texture, shadows, mockups, borders, grids, extra panels, watermark.
A genuinely different concept from a geometric cubic-cell logo: this one is entirely smooth, flowing and organic in contour.
```
